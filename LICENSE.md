# Licenses

The samples in this repository are derived from two sources. Each instrument folder's `manifest.json` also records its license.

## CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): `steinway-b`, `kawai-grand`, `upright`

Derived from the [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), which is released under CC0 1.0 Universal: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. No attribution is required; the following is given as a courtesy.

- Steinway B grand: VCSL, `Chordophones/Zithers/Grand Piano, Steinway B`.
- Kawai grand: VCSL, `Chordophones/Zithers/Grand Piano, Kawai`.
- Upright piano (Knight): VCSL, `Chordophones/Zithers/Upright Piano, Knight`; sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio for VSCO 2 Pro.

The files here were trimmed, faded, gain-adjusted where noted in the README, down-mixed to mono and encoded to AAC.

## CC BY 3.0: `yamaha-c5`

Derived from the [Salamander Grand Piano V3](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano) (Yamaha C5), recorded by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Attribution is required: credit Alexander Holm and link to the license.

Changes: a subset of 30 notes and 4 of the 16 original velocity layers, down-mixed to mono and encoded to AAC (.m4a) at 96 kbps, renamed to the `<midi>_<layer>.m4a` scheme.
